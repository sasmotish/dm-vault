# The Charred Road

> [!quote] Read aloud
> The road climbs between dead trees. The higher you go, the thicker the ash: soon it covers your boots, and every step raises a grey cloud.

## Weather

Check the weather in the top bar before the party leaves (set it to *Temperate*, *Autumn*). If the effect mentions visibility or travel, apply it here. In fog or heavy rain, the party arrives at the mine without seeing the lookouts.

## Random encounters

Roll a d6 once during the climb: on a 1 or 2, roll on the table below.

| d8 | Encounter |
|---|---|
| 1 | A burnt cart, its oxen gone. In the back: 2d10 sp and a torn map of the mine. |
| 2-3 | Two [[Goblin Warrior\|Cinder Goblins]] dragging a sheep. They flee toward the mine. |
| 4-5 | A hot spring of ash: DC 11 Dexterity save or take 1d6 fire damage. |
| 6 | The skeleton of a horse, completely picked clean. Tracks of small bare feet around it. |
| 7 | The mayor's son's lost boot. #clue |
| 8 | A distant roar. Everyone makes a DC 10 Wisdom save or becomes [[Frightened\|frightened]] until the end of their next turn. |

> [!player] The tracks
> Small bare footprints, dozens of them, all going up toward the mine. Some are dragging something heavy.

## The ash trap

Near the top, the goblins dug a pit and covered it with ash. The first character in line makes a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check. On a failure, they fall 10 feet and take 1d6 bludgeoning damage, and the goblin lookout shoots its shortbow. *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.
