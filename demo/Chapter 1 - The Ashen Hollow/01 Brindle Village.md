# Brindle Village

![[map-ashen-hollow.jpg|600]]

> [!quote] Read aloud
> Grey flakes drift down on the road like early snow, but they are warm, and they smell of smoke. Brindle is a few dozen stone houses around a well. Every shutter is closed. At the end of the only street, a lantern burns above the door of an inn: *The Rusty Flagon*.

## Arrival

The villagers watch from behind their shutters. A boy runs to fetch [[Mayor Aldric Venn]], who arrives out of breath a few minutes later. Anyone who looks closely at the ash sees tiny ==glowing embers== in it. A DC 12 Intelligence (Nature) check reveals that it is not from a forest fire: it is too fine, and nothing is burning in the valley.

> [!player] The ash
> The ash is warm to the touch and too fine to come from burning wood. It falls from the north, where the old mine is.

## The Rusty Flagon

The inn is half empty. The innkeeper, a heavy man named Tobbin, serves thin ale for 4 cp and a bowl of stew for 1 sp. Anyone who buys a round for the room (5 sp) hears the rumours below. #clue

> [!tip] Running the tavern
> Roll `1d4` for how many rumours the party hears tonight, then read them from [[Rumours]] in order.

## The mayor's offer

[[Mayor Aldric Venn|The mayor]] offers **400 gp** to "whoever makes the ash stop". He can pay 100 gp up front. If the party haggles (DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)), he goes up to 500 gp but cannot pay anything in advance.

> [!player]- The mayor's letter of employment
> ![[handout-letter.jpg]]

## Bessa's shop

The only shop in town belongs to [[Bessa Thornquill]]. Her prices are in her note: click her name to keep it open while the players shop.

> [!warning] If the party asks about the old mine
> Everyone in Brindle knows where it is: two hours up the [[02 The Charred Road|Charred Road]]. Nobody will guide them there.

## Leaving Brindle

When the party sets off, go to [[02 The Charred Road]].
