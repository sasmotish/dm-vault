# Chapter 1: The Ashen Hollow

The farming village of Brindle sits at the edge of a valley where nothing grows any more. Ash falls with the autumn leaves, the sheep go missing, and the mayor pays well for anyone willing to find out why. The truth: an [[Adult Red Dragon]] has claimed the old mine above the valley, and a tribe of goblins now serves it.

> [!abstract] The chapter in one sentence
> The party arrives in Brindle, follows the ash up the Charred Road, and faces the dragon in its lair, or finds a way to make it leave.
