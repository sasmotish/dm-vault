# Rumours

> [!player] Rumour 1: the sheep
> Old Mag has lost nine sheep this month. No wolf tracks, just scorched grass where they were.

> [!player] Rumour 2: the lights
> At night, from the top of the hill, you can see a red glow inside the old mine.

> [!player] Rumour 3: the little thieves
> Small figures with blackened faces were seen stealing chickens. They ran off toward the mine, laughing.

> [!player] Rumour 4: the mayor
> The mayor's son went up the Charred Road ten days ago and never came back. The mayor does not talk about it.
