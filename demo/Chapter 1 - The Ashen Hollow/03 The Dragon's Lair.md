# The Dragon's Lair

> [!quote] Read aloud
> The mine entrance breathes heat like an open oven. Inside, the walls are glazed black, as if the rock itself had melted. Deep below, something enormous is breathing.

## The goblin guard room

Four [[Goblin Warrior|goblins]] play dice around a brazier. If the party was not seen on the road, they are surprised.

> [!example] Combat 1: the guard room
> 1. Show the swords (initiative bar) in the top bar.
> 2. Add the players with their totals (switch to *Player*).
> 3. Switch to *Monster*, type `gob` and pick *Goblin Warrior* in the suggestions, add ` x4`, and leave the initiative box **empty**: each goblin rolls its own initiative and gets its HP from its note.
> 4. Press *Next*: on a goblin's turn, its stat block pins itself on the right, and its image appears on the player screen if it is open.
> 5. Damage a goblin in the *In combat* box of the pin: type `-5` and Enter.
> 6. Give a goblin a condition with the tag icon, for example Prone for 1 round.
> 7. At the end, press *End*: the goblins leave, the players stay.

> [!player] The goblins' dice
> The goblins were playing with dice made of small bones. One of them is carved with a dragon's eye.

## The prisoner

Chained in a side room: Pell, the mayor's son, alive but weak. He knows the dragon sleeps in the great hall and wakes at the slightest sound. #clue

> [!player] What Pell whispers
> "It's asleep, but it hears everything. The goblins bring it gold every night. It wants the whole valley."

## The great hall

The [[Adult Red Dragon]] sleeps on the hoard. Sneaking past it: DC 20 Dexterity (Stealth), with disadvantage in metal armor.

> [!danger] Combat 2: the dragon
> This fight is deadly for a level 3 to 5 party. Give them a way out: the dragon is vain and can be bargained with, and it would rather take tribute than burn the village it wants to rule.
> Add `Adult Red Dragon` to the initiative with an empty box. On its turn, remember its legendary actions and roll its breath recharge: (Recharge 5–6).

> [!player] The dragon's offer
> "Little ones. Bring me a tithe of gold each new moon, and Brindle may keep its roofs."

## Treasure

- 2,400 gp and 3,100 sp in loose coins
- A silver circlet worth 250 gp
- A *potion of fire resistance*
- The deed to the old mine, signed by the mayor's grandfather

## Ending the chapter

If the dragon is killed or bargained with, the ash stops falling within three days. The mayor pays what he promised. Pell mentions a cloaked stranger who visited the dragon before it came: a hook for [[The Ashen Order]].
