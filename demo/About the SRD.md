# About the SRD

The monsters in the *Monsters* folder and the conditions in *Rules/Conditions* come from the **System Reference Document 5.2** (the free rules of the 2024 edition of D&D, "5.5e").

> [!quote] Attribution
> This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2 ("SRD 5.2") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The data was converted from the open 5e-database project (https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database, MIT License), a community transcription of the SRD. Changes: converted to Markdown notes with properties for DM Vault, conditions linked to their rule notes, spell lists laid out as lists. The Initiative of each monster is calculated from its Dexterity (the data does not include it): check it against the book for monsters that add their proficiency bonus.

Keep this note (and the source line at the bottom of each monster) if you share or publish these files.
