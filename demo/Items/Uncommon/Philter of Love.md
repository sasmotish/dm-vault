# Philter of Love

The next time you see a creature within 10 minutes after drinking this philter, you are [[Charmed|charmed]] by that creature and have the Charmed condition for 1 hour.

This rose-hued, effervescent liquid contains one easy-to-miss bubble shaped like a heart.
