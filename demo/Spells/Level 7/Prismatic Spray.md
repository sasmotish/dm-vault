# Prismatic Spray

Eight rays of light flash from you in a 60-foot Cone. Each creature in the Cone makes a Dexterity saving throw. For each target, roll 1d8 to determine which color ray affects it, consulting the Prismatic Rays table. Prismatic Rays 1d8 Ray 2    Orange. Failed Save: 12d6 Acid damage. Successful Save: Half as much damage. 4    Green. Failed Save: 12d6 Poison damage. Successful Save: Half as much damage. 1d8 Ray 6    Indigo. Failed Save: The target has the [[Restrained]] condition and makes a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If it successfully saves three times, the condition ends. If it fails three times, it has the [[Petrified]] condition until it is freed by an effect like the Greater Restoration spell. The successes and failures needn't be consecutive; keep track of both until the target collects three of a kind. 8    Special. The target is struck by two rays. Roll twice, rerolling any 8.
