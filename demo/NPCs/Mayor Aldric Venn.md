# Mayor Aldric Venn

A thin, balding man in a coat that used to be good. He has not slept in days and it shows.

## At the table

- Offers 400 gp, 100 up front (see [[01 Brindle Village#The mayor's offer]]).
- Avoids any question about his son. DC 14 Wisdom (Insight): he feels guilty, not just sad.
- If the party brings Pell back alive, he adds 100 gp and the gratitude of the whole village.

> [!player] What the mayor says
> "The ash started a month ago. The crops, the sheep... Please. Whatever is up there, make it stop."
