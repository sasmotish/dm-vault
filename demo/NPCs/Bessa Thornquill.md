# Bessa Thornquill

A round, cheerful halfling who has run the only shop in Brindle for thirty years. Since the ash began, business is bad, and she is ready to haggle.

## Inventory

| Item | Price | In stock |
|---|---|---|
| Rope, hempen (50 ft) | 1 gp | 1d4 |
| Rations (1 day) | 5 sp | 2d6 |
| Torch | 1 cp | 3d6 |
| Healing potion | 50 gp | 1d3 |
| Flask of oil | 1 sp | 1d6 |
| Wet cloak (keeps ash away) | 2 gp | 1d4 |
| Potion of fire resistance | 300 gp | 1 |

## Haggling

- DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion): 10% off.
- DC 16: 20% off, and she throws in a torch.
- Mentioning the dragon, or promising to deal with the ash: automatic 10% off.

> [!player] Bessa's advice
> "Up the mine, petal? Wet your cloaks before you go. Ash doesn't stick to wet wool, and you'll breathe easier."
